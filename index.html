RESILIENCE OS AI is an emergency intelligence prototype designed to detect incidents, assess risk, understand impact, provide response recommendations, communicate incidents through voice and monitor recovery.

The Problem

During emergencies, information is often fragmented, delayed and difficult to understand.

Delayed Detection

Emergency situations may not be identified quickly enough.

Information Overload

Responders and decision makers may receive too much unstructured information.

Communication Barriers

During stressful situations, not everyone can easily read or process dashboard information.

Our Solution

One intelligence layer connecting detection, analysis, location, communication and recovery.

Detect

Collect incident information and sensor signals.

Analyze

Assess severity, risk and potential impact.

Respond

Generate recommended response actions.

Communicate

Deliver incident information visually and through voice.

Locate

Display incident and user location on an interactive map.

Recover

Monitor recovery progress until completion.
