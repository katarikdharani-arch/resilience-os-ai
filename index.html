AI-POWERED EMERGENCY INTELLIGENCE

Sense. Understand. Respond.

RESILIENCE OS AI is a prototype emergency intelligence platform that combines sensor signals, incident analysis, location intelligence and recovery monitoring into one unified system.

The Problem

During emergencies, information can be fragmented, delayed and difficult to interpret.

Delayed Detection

Emergency situations may not be identified quickly enough.

Information Overload

Multiple signals and reports make it difficult to understand the real situation.

Poor Coordination

Critical information may be spread across different systems.

Recovery Blind Spots

After the initial response, progress may be difficult to monitor.

Our Solution

A unified emergency intelligence workflow designed to transform raw incident information into actionable insights.

01 — Sense

Collect sensor and incident signals.

02 — Understand

Analyze incident type, severity and potential impact.

03 — Respond

Generate recommended response actions and route intelligence.

04 — Recover

Track recovery progress until the situation reaches a stable state.

Neo 1 — Intelligent Edge Device

Neo 1 represents the sensing layer of the RESILIENCE OS AI architecture.


It can conceptually monitor environmental, motion and emergency signals and send those signals to the intelligence platform.
