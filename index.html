RESILIENCE OS AI combines wearable hardware, sensor signals and artificial intelligence to understand risky situations and coordinate faster emergency responses.

The Problem

⚠️ Risk Goes Unnoticed

Important signals from people, devices and environments can be missed during critical situations.

📡 Scattered Signals

Sensor information may exist in different systems without a unified intelligence layer.

⏱️ Slow Response

Delays in understanding an incident can lead to delays in taking the right action.

Our Solution

📡 Sense

Capture relevant signals through Neo 1 and the prototype sensor layer.

🧠 Understand

AI analyzes available signals and context to estimate the situation and risk level.

🚨 Respond

The system presents alerts, incident details and recommended response actions.
