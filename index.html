RESILIENCE OS AI is a prototype emergency intelligence platform designed to collect incident information, assess risk, understand potential impact, provide response recommendations and monitor recovery progress.

The Problem

During emergencies, information is often fragmented, delayed and difficult to understand. Decision makers need a faster way to organize available information and prioritize response.

Delayed Information

Emergency information may arrive from different sources at different times, making rapid assessment difficult.

Information Overload

Responders can receive multiple signals and reports without an immediate understanding of what matters most.

Recovery Visibility

After the initial emergency, teams still need to track the transition from incident detection to recovery.

Our Solution

One intelligence workflow for incident understanding, risk prioritization, location awareness and recovery tracking.

01

Sense

Collect incident information and location signals.

02

Understand

Assess incident type, risk level and potential impact.

03

Respond

Generate recommended response actions and route intelligence.

04

Recover

Track recovery progress until the incident is marked recovered.

Neo 1

Neo 1 represents the edge intelligence component of RESILIENCE OS AI. It can conceptually act as a sensing device that provides emergency signals to the intelligence platform.


The prototype demonstrates how physical-world signals can be connected to a centralized emergency intelligence workflow.
